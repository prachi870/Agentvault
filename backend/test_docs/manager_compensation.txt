Manager Compensation Structure (CONFIDENTIAL - Managers and Above)

BASE SALARY RANGES
- Entry Manager: $85,000 - $110,000
- Senior Manager: $110,000 - $140,000
- Director: $140,000 - $180,000

PERFORMANCE BONUS
- Target bonus: 15% of base salary
- Range: 0% to 30% based on performance
- Paid quarterly

STOCK OPTIONS
- Grant schedule: Annual review
- Vesting: 4-year period with 1-year cliff
- Exercise window: 10 years

ADDITIONAL BENEFITS
- Executive health plan upgrade
- Car allowance: $800/month
- Home office setup: $3,000 one-time
- Additional PTO: 5 extra days per year

PROMOTION CRITERIA
- Minimum 2 years in current role
- Performance rating: Exceeds Expectations or higher
- Business justification required
- Budget approval needed
